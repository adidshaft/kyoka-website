## Description

Your strap. Your data.

Atria is a private, local-first companion for a compatible strap. It connects directly over Bluetooth and turns the signals already on your wrist into a clear picture of recovery, sleep, strain, workouts and long-term trends.

NO ATRIA ACCOUNT OR CLOUD

Atria processes and stores your health and fitness history on your iPhone. There is no Atria login, backend profile, advertising SDK or analytics tracker. Optional anonymous research sharing stays off until you inspect the bundle and agree.

ONE PURCHASE, NOT ANOTHER SUBSCRIPTION

Buy Atria once. There is no recurring Atria subscription.

READINESS WITH CONTEXT

See recovery, sleep and strain together. Atria shows where a metric came from, whether it is still learning and what evidence is missing instead of filling gaps with fake precision.

LIVE VITALS AND WORKOUTS

Follow live heart rate, heart-rate zones and strain while you move. Save local workout evidence and revisit it whenever you want.

PERSONAL TRENDS

Explore day, week and month views for HRV and other supported metrics. Atria builds a personal baseline from your own local history.

JOURNAL WHAT CHANGED

Record habits and daily context, then compare them against the signals Atria can support. Your notes stay local too.

BUILT FOR HONEST SIGNALS

Atria keeps learning and unavailable states visible. Hardware compatibility, firmware and signal quality can affect which values are available.

Atria is a personal fitness and research companion, not a medical device. Do not use it for diagnosis, treatment or safety-critical decisions.
