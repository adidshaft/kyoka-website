Privacy policy

Your health data stays yours.

Atria is designed to work locally on your iPhone. It does not require an Atria account or send your strap and health data to an Atria backend.

Effective August 14, 2026 · Atria 1.0

Summary Data on your device Collection and sharing Apple permissions Your control Contact

Summary

Atria does not collect, sell or share your personal health and fitness data. The app has no Atria account, cloud database, advertising SDK or third-party analytics tracker.

This policy describes the information Atria processes locally, the iOS permissions it may request and the actions that can move data outside the app at your direction.

Information processed on your device

Depending on the features you use and the data your compatible strap makes available, Atria may process and store:

  • Your nickname, profile settings and training preferences.
  • Bluetooth connection information, strap identifiers and battery status.
  • Sensor readings such as heart rate, beat intervals and supported motion or physiological signals.
  • Derived personal-fitness information such as resting heart rate, HRV, recovery, strain, sleep summaries, workout summaries and trends.
  • Journal entries, confirmations, edits and other context you add.
  • Local diagnostics needed to explain data quality, confidence and connection behavior.

This information is stored in Atria's app container on your iPhone. Atria's developer cannot remotely view it.

No developer collection, advertising or tracking

Atria does not operate a backend service for the app. It does not upload your profile, strap readings, metrics, journal entries or diagnostics to the developer. It does not sell data, build advertising profiles, use cross-app tracking or include advertising.

The App Store and iOS may process purchase, installation and system diagnostic information under Apple's own policies. Atria does not receive your payment-card details.

Bluetooth, Health and system permissions

Bluetooth

Atria uses Bluetooth to discover and communicate directly with a compatible strap nearby. Bluetooth data is processed locally.

Health

If you enable supported Apple Health features, iOS presents a system permission sheet. Atria accesses only the categories you approve and uses them only for the feature described in the app. You can change this access at any time in iOS Settings or the Health app. Atria does not send Apple Health data to the developer.

Notifications and background activity

If enabled, iOS may allow Atria to deliver local reminders or continue permitted Bluetooth work. These permissions do not create an Atria cloud account.

Exports, deletion and your control

Data can leave Atria only when you take an action that uses another app or service—for example, exporting a backup, sharing a file, writing an authorized item to Apple Health or including information in a support message. Once shared, that destination's privacy policy applies.

You can remove Atria's local data using controls available in the app or by deleting the app from your iPhone. Deleting the app does not remove copies you previously exported or information managed independently by Apple.

Health and safety

Atria is a personal fitness and research companion, not a medical device. Its metrics can be incomplete, estimated or affected by hardware and signal quality. Do not use Atria for diagnosis, treatment or safety-critical decisions.

Atria is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to the developer in a support request, contact us so it can be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If Atria's data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated. A material change that introduces developer collection would also be reflected in the App Store privacy disclosure before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact @adidshaft on X or visit Atria Support.
