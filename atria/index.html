Built for ownership

Your strap should keep working for you.

Atria gives compatible hardware a useful second life. It reads the signals your strap broadcasts, builds a personal baseline and makes uncertainty visible instead of inventing precision.

No feed. No ads. No cloud profile built around your body.

Local means local

Atria does not collect your health data.

The app has no Atria account, backend, advertising SDK or analytics tracker. Strap readings, derived metrics, journal entries and settings are processed and stored on your device. Data leaves the app only when you deliberately use an iOS feature such as an export or an authorized Health integration.
